# MyNote — source of truth

Everything generated for a store listing or a social post draws from this file.
If a claim is not here, it does not go in an ad.

## One line

Your notes, in your storage, in a theme you chose.

## What it is

A block editor for iPhone, iPad and Android. Type a page the way you would in
Notion — headings, to-dos, lists, quotes, code — except it opens instantly, works
with no connection and no account, and lets you design how it looks.

## What is true about it

These are the checkable claims. Use these.

- **Works with no connection.** Every edit saves to the device first. On a plane,
  on the Tube, in a basement: identical behaviour.
- **Works with no account.** Open it and write. Sign-in exists only for syncing
  and for carrying a purchase to another device.
- **Native on both platforms.** SwiftUI on iOS, Jetpack Compose on Android. Not a
  web view in a wrapper.
- **Themes you build.** Colours for light *and* dark, text size, line height,
  heading weight, corner radius, spacing, page width. Previewed live against a
  real page.
- **No servers, no account.** Backups go to a MyNote folder in your own Google
  Drive or iCloud Drive. You can open it, read it and delete it yourself. We
  never see your notes.
- **One purchase covers both platforms.** Buy on iPhone; connect the same Google
  Drive on Android and it is already unlocked.
- **Backup keeps working offline.** Edits are saved on the device and uploaded
  when you reconnect — including after a force-quit.
- **Two devices editing at once merge predictably**, rather than one silently
  overwriting the other.
- **Adapts to the screen.** One pane on a phone, two on a tablet or unfolded
  foldable.
- **Free tier is a complete app.** Unlimited notes, every block type, three
  themes, search. No trial timer.

## What is NOT true — never claim these

- ~~End-to-end encrypted~~ — Google and Apple can read files in their own
  storage, as with any file you put there.
- ~~iCloud syncs to Android~~ — **it does not.** Apple publishes no iCloud Drive
  API for Android. Saying otherwise earns refunds. Google Drive is the
  cross-platform option.
- ~~Real-time collaboration~~ — backup is between *your* devices. No shared editing.
- ~~AI features~~ — none.
- ~~Web app~~ — iOS and Android only.
- ~~Import from Notion / Evernote~~ — not built.
- ~~Attachments are backed up~~ — images are local-only for now.

## Pricing, stated plainly

- Free: unlimited notes, all block types, three themes, search.
- **MyNote Pro: $14.99, once.** Custom themes and cloud backup. No subscription.

## Who it is for

People who write for themselves, keep a phone and a tablet, and have been
irritated by a note app that spun a loading indicator over their own words.

## The strongest demo

The theme editor, on camera. Dragging a colour and watching a real page change
under it is the single most watchable thing the app does. Lead every video with
it.

## Second strongest

Open the Drive app and show the MyNote folder — the actual files, sitting in the
viewer's own storage. "No account. No subscription. Your notes, your Drive."
That is the claim no competitor can make, so lead the no-server angle with it.

## Third

Airplane mode on. Type a page. Airplane mode off. Watch it appear on the other
device. Fifteen seconds, no narration needed.
