MyNote is a block editor for people who would rather not hand their notes to somebody else's server.

There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Open the app and write.

WRITTEN IN BLOCKS
Every paragraph, heading, list item and quote is its own block. Press Return to start a new one. Backspace at the very start of a block folds it into the one above, so a stray keystroke never strands your text.

Headings, bulleted and numbered lists, to-dos you can tick off, quotes, code and dividers — all reachable from a bar that sits above the keyboard, so you are never hunting through menus mid-sentence.

FORMATTING INSIDE A LINE
Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and links apply to part of a line rather than to the whole block. Formatting travels with the words: split a block and each half keeps its own, merge two and both survive.

WORKS WITHOUT A CONNECTION
Everything is saved on your device the moment you type it. There is no spinner, no "saving...", and nothing to wait for. A tunnel, a plane or a dead signal makes no difference.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PHONE
MyNote has no servers, so there is nowhere for your notes to be sent. No account, no analytics, no advertising identifier. Your notes stay on your device.

MAKE IT YOURS
Three built-in themes, or build your own: colours for light and dark, text size, line height, spacing, corners and page width. Every screen follows it.
